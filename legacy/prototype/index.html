Methodology

How INR Rating Works

FSSAI Indian Nutrition Rating framework mechanics.

1

Baseline Points (Risks)

Calculates penalty points from Energy, Saturated Fat, Total Sugars, and Sodium levels per 100g.

2

Positive Points (Benefits)

Awards points for healthy Fruit, Vegetable, Nut % (FVL%), Dietary Fibre, and Protein content.

3

Star Rating Conversion

Net Score = (Baseline Points) - (Positive Points). Score strictly maps to 0.5 to 5.0 Star visual badge.
